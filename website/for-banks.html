What the check does

The mandate says which agent, from which accounts, to which payees, how much and until when. It is signed by a signatory, alone or jointly.

Before you execute, the checker verifies the signatures against your own KYB records, checks revocation and limits, and signs its answer. That record works as proof later.

Why the bank’s records

Trust comes from what you already know: your KYB and the keys you enrolled for each signatory. An LEI or vLEI is optional, for cross-bank cases. The mandate alone never makes someone a signatory.

A changed bank account on a known payee is blocked. That is the scam agents fall for first.

Why now

“…proving that an agent has the authority to pay on the buyer’s behalf.”Governor Christopher Waller, Federal Reserve, Sibos 2026

€2.2bn was lost to credit transfer fraud in the EEA in 2024 (EBA and ECB). Businesses reported $3.05bn in email payment fraud to the FBI for 2025 (IC3). A mandate does not catch all of that. It does stop an agent paying someone the company never approved.

How you try it

Shadow mode: the check runs next to your flow, on test data or copies. It has no access to funds and no effect on your payments. You run the open-source checker yourself, or use a hosted test setup.

It maps to ISO 20022 pain.001, and Python and TypeScript checkers pass the same test vectors. Nobody runs it in production yet.
