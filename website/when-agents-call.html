A voice says it’s calling for your customer. Detection tells you it’s a bot. It doesn’t tell you the customer sent it, or what it may agree to.

What a mandate link shows

The customer signs a short statement: which agent, toward which company, what it may do, up to how much, until when. The agent gives your team the link and reads out a short code.

Your team opens the link. The page checks the signature in the browser and shows the scope and the code to match. No account and no integration.

Create one and try it

What it doesn’t prove

On its own, a link proves that the holder of a key signed this scope. It doesn’t prove that the holder is your customer. For that you still tie the key to someone you know, for example during your normal verification.

Whether it satisfies your rules is a question for your compliance team and counsel. We make no claim that it does.

Where it stands

The link format is an experiment and may change. Nobody uses it in production. It is not for health data as it stands: a plan or clinic would need its own review first.
