What happens today

Most teams either block agents or treat them like the customer. Consent, if there is any, sits inside each company’s own flow and doesn’t travel with the agent.

What changes with a mandate

The customer signs what the agent may do, for example compare quotes but not change a policy. Your team opens a link, matches a short code, and reads exactly what was signed. No account and no integration.

What it doesn’t do

A link alone proves that the holder of a key signed this. It doesn’t prove who that person is, so you still confirm identity as you do now, or tie the key to someone you know. Whether it meets your rules is a question for your compliance team and counsel. We make no claim that it does. Full list.
