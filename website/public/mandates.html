Questions

What is Gleipnir?

A power of attorney for AI agents. A person or company signs what one agent may do: for whom, toward whom, how much, until when. The other side can check it before acting.

What does “Know Your Agent” mean?

Banks already know their customers (KYC). Know Your Agent means also knowing who an AI agent acts for and what it is allowed to do. More on Know Your Agent.

Do you see our data?

No. A mandate link is checked in the browser, and a payment check can run on your own systems without calling us. We never touch money.

Isn’t this what bot detection does?

Detection tells you it’s a bot. It doesn’t tell you who sent it or what it may do. Visa and Mastercard have agent programmes, but only for card payments.

We already set limits in our app. Why this?

Those limits only work inside that app. A mandate is signed by the person it comes from, and anyone can check it.

Does it stop an agent making mistakes?

No. It proves who allowed what. A fake invoice from an approved supplier, or a bad call inside the allowed scope, isn’t something a mandate can catch. What it doesn’t do.

Does it prove who signed?

Only if the checker knows the key. A bank uses its own records of who may sign. A mandate link alone proves that the holder of a key signed this, nothing more.

Is it legally binding?

We don’t know yet. It will differ by country and by what the agent does, so ask your counsel.

Can I use it today?

The specification, the checkers, an MCP server and the demos are open source under Apache 2.0. We have no production customers yet.
