Why we exist

A robot can’t learn a job it has never seen.

  1. Why

    Robots are coming to real jobs. But almost nobody films skilled people at work, so robots learn from staged demos and household chores.

  2. How

    A small camera on the worker’s head during a normal paid job. A signed agreement and pay for every hour. Faces blurred, video cut into single tasks.

  3. What

    Clear first-person video of real work, with a label for every task, ready for robot training.

Vision

Robots that can do real jobs, learned from the people who do them best.

Mission

Film how skilled people really work and turn it into data robots can learn from, fairly for the people on camera.

How it works

From a normal working day to training data.

  1. 01 / RECORD

    On the job

    A head-mounted camera on a professional during paid work. Both hands in frame, a signed release per session.

  2. 02 / CLEAN

    Privacy and QC

    Faces and readable text blurred, audio removed, blurry or empty footage dropped. Processed locally; raw video never leaves our machines.

  3. 03 / CUT

    Tasks and cards

    Full sessions cut into tasks with start and end times, plus a JSON card per clip: task, hands, rework.

  4. 04 / DELIVER

    In your format

    MP4 (HEVC) and JSON by default. Non-exclusive licence, or exclusive sets and recording to your task list.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask.

What is first-person (egocentric) video for robots?

Video recorded from a camera on the worker’s head, so it shows exactly what the worker sees and does with their hands. Robot and AI teams use it to teach models how real tasks are done.

What do you record today?

Professional electricians doing paid work in Slovakia: pulling cable, chasing walls, setting boxes, preparing and connecting wires, mounting sockets and switches, wiring panels, testing and rework. More people, trades and workplaces are being added.

What does the data look like?

4K video (3840×2880, 4:3, 30 fps) from a head-mounted camera, both hands in frame. Full sessions plus clips cut per task, each with a JSON card (task, duration, hands, rework). MP4 (HEVC) by default.

How do you handle privacy and consent?

Every worker signs a written release for every session and is paid for every recorded hour. Faces and readable text are blurred and audio is removed before delivery.

How much does it cost?

Samples are free. Non-exclusive footage starts at $25 per hour of footage that passes our checks, cheaper above 100 hours. Exclusive sets and recording to your task list are priced on request.

Can you record custom tasks?

Yes. Send the task list, volume and format. We can film to your spec, add other trades and workplaces, and move to stereo cameras with motion sensors.

How do I get a sample?

Write to us for a free sample. We reply within a day.

Where are you based?

Vienna, Austria. We film in Central Europe, starting in Slovakia.
