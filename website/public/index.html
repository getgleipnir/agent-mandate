Why it is different

Most egocentric data is household chores from one region. Skilled work on real sites is scarce.

Skilled, tool-heavy tasks

Fine manipulation with pliers, screwdrivers, strippers and testers, on objects that change state. The kind of work humanoids are being built for.

Real conditions, not a set

Dust, bad light, ladders, awkward angles, interruptions and rework. Nothing is rehearsed for the camera.

European sites

Different buildings, standards and tools from the data that floods the market today. Diversity of place is what buyers keep asking for.

Delivery

Full jobs plus per-task clips, with a card for every clip.

  • Full sessions and task clips. Each job from start to finish, and cut into tasks with start and end times.
  • Privacy handled. Faces and readable text blurred before delivery. Audio removed unless you ask for it.
  • Consent on paper. Written release from each worker for every session, and permission from the contractor. Available for inspection.
  • Standard formats. MP4 (HEVC), JSON sidecar per clip. Other formats on request.
  • Your licence. Non-exclusive commercial training by default. Exclusive sets and recording to your spec on request.
