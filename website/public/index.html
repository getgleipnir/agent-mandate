Why we exist

A robot can’t learn a job it has never seen.

  1. Why

    Robots are coming to real jobs. But almost nobody films skilled people at work, so robots learn from staged demos and household chores.

  2. How

    A small camera on the worker’s head during a normal paid job. A signed agreement and pay for every hour. Faces blurred, video cut into single tasks.

  3. What

    Clear first-person video of real work, with a label for every task, ready for robot training.

Vision

Robots that can do real jobs, learned from the people who do them best.

Mission

Film how skilled people really work and turn it into data robots can learn from, fairly for the people on camera.

How it works

From a normal working day to training data.

  1. 01 / RECORD

    On the job

    A head-mounted camera on a professional during paid work. Both hands in frame, a signed release per session.

  2. 02 / CLEAN

    Privacy and QC

    Faces and readable text blurred, audio removed, blurry or empty footage dropped. Processed locally; raw video never leaves our machines.

  3. 03 / CUT

    Tasks and cards

    Full sessions cut into tasks with start and end times, plus a JSON card per clip: task, hands, rework.

  4. 04 / DELIVER

    In your format

    MP4 (HEVC) and JSON by default. Non-exclusive licence, or exclusive sets and recording to your task list.
