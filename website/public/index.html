Why we exist

Robots will learn the trades from the people who know them.

Vision

Robots that do real work on real job sites, trained by tradespeople, with tradespeople sharing in the value.

Mission

Record how professionals actually work, with their consent, and turn it into data robots can learn from.

Principle 01 · Real work only

Paid jobs on live sites. Dust, ladders, bad light, mistakes and rework stay in. Nothing is staged for the camera.

Principle 02 · Consent first

Every worker signs for every session. Faces and text are blurred before anything leaves us. We are building a share of every sale for the people on camera.

The gap

Robots learn from data. The trades have almost none.

Language models had the internet. Robots have lab demos and a flood of household clips. The skilled, tool-heavy work humanoids are being built for happens on job sites nobody films.

  1. 01

    Lab data is clean and narrow

    Same tables, same objects, same light. Policies break on the first real site.

  2. 02

    Bulk video is chores from one region

    Folding and dishes in homes. Little fine tool work, little variety of place.

  3. 03

    Skilled work is hard to reach

    You need trust with contractors and workers to film a real job. That is our access.

  4. 04

    Consent is an afterthought

    Most footage carries thin releases. Buyers need rights they can defend.

How it works

From a working day to a training-ready dataset.

  1. 01 / RECORD

    On the job

    A head-mounted camera on a professional during paid work. Both hands in frame, a signed release per session.

  2. 02 / CLEAN

    Privacy and QC

    Faces and readable text blurred, audio removed, blurry or empty footage dropped. Processed locally; raw video never leaves our machines.

  3. 03 / CUT

    Tasks and cards

    Full sessions cut into tasks with start and end times, plus a JSON card per clip: task, hands, rework.

  4. 04 / DELIVER

    In your format

    MP4 (HEVC) and JSON by default. Non-exclusive licence, or exclusive sets and recording to your task list.
