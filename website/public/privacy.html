Privacy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Who is responsible

Ivan Voloshyn, Vienna, Austria. Email: ivan.voloshyn@getgleipnir.com

What this website collects

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties. The examples on the homepage and the demo run entirely in your browser and send nothing. A mandate link keeps its contents after the # in the address, which browsers do not send to our server. If a link uses our status address, opening it asks our server whether it was revoked, so our host sees your IP address and which status address was asked. Revoking sends a random id, a public key and a signature. We store only that the id was revoked and when.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. When you visit, Netlify processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request to deliver the page and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Netlify may process this data in the United States. See Netlify’s privacy policy at netlify.com/privacy.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We don’t share it or add you to a mailing list. We delete it when it is no longer needed for the conversation.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict or object to its processing, and ask for a copy. Write to the address above. You can also complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde, dsb.gv.at).
