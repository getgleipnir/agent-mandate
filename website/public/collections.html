What happens today

Collectors have started asking how to treat these calls: is the agent the consumer, or a third party? Most handle it case by case.

What changes with a mandate

The consumer signs which agent may talk to you, what it may agree to, and up to how much. Your team opens a link, matches a short code, and reads exactly what was signed. No account and no integration.

What it doesn’t do

A link alone proves that the holder of a key signed this. It doesn’t prove who that person is, so you still confirm identity as you do now, or tie the key to someone you know. Whether it meets your rules is a question for your compliance team and counsel. We make no claim that it does. Full list.

Where it stands
