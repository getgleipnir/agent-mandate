Technical details 

    

  


  
The check runs in this browser. The link carries the whole mandate after the #, which browsers do not send to any server. If the mandate names a status URL, this page asks that server whether the mandate was revoked, which tells its owner your IP address and the time; otherwise nothing is sent anywhere.

  
A valid signature proves that the holder of the signing key approved exactly this scope, and nothing outside it. It does not prove who that holder is: the name and any domain in the link are typed by the signer. Until the key is enrolled with a bank or an identity provider, confirm the person's identity as you normally would. It does not make the agent's decisions correct.
