Mandate link

An AI agent calls a company for you: a debt collector, an insurer, your bank. Say here what it may do, sign it, and give the agent the link. Whoever it calls opens the link and sees, in seconds, what you allowed and that it has not been changed.

Nothing is uploaded when you sign. Only if you revoke does this page send a random id, a public key and a signature. The whole signed mandate is inside the link, after the #. The other side sees your name as you type it here: the link does not prove who you are.
