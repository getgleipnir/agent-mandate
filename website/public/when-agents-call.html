What your team sees

The customer signs a short statement: which agent, toward which company, what it may do, up to how much, until when. The agent gives your team the link and reads out a short code. Your team opens the link and sees the scope and the code to match. No account and no integration.

What it doesn’t prove

On its own, a link proves that the holder of a key signed this scope. It doesn’t prove that the holder is your customer. For that you tie the key to someone you know, or confirm identity as you do now. Whether it satisfies your rules is a question for your compliance team and counsel. We make no claim that it does.

Where it stands

The link format is an experiment and may change. Nobody uses it in production. Don’t use it for health data without your own review.
