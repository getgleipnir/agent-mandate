What exists today

Card networks are building agent checks for card purchases. Payment apps have limits that work inside the app. Agent protocols are starting to define how an agent proves its key.

Bank transfers, phone calls and portals have no shared way to check who sent an agent. Protocols such as PAP and PACT leave signed approvals and revocation open.

Where we fit

We work on the second and third questions: one signed mandate the other side can check, usable next to whatever identifies the agent. We don’t verify identities: a bank’s KYB, a register or a vLEI issuer does that.

The format is an open specification (version 0.6, draft) with Python and TypeScript checkers. We also work in the open with the Decentralized Identity Foundation’s groups on agent authority.

We test one of your flows with you, free

Shadow mode: the check runs next to your flow, with no access to funds and no effect on what you do today. We are early and want to learn from real cases.
