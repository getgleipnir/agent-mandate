Technical details 

    

  


  
The check runs in this browser. The link carries the whole mandate after the #, which browsers do not send to any server. If the mandate names a status URL, this page asks it whether the mandate was revoked; otherwise nothing is sent anywhere.

  
A valid signature proves that the holder of the signing key approved exactly this scope, and nothing outside it. It does not prove who that holder is, unless the key is enrolled with a bank or an identity provider; confirm the person's identity as you normally would. It does not make the agent's decisions correct.
