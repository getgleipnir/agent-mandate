Mandate link

An AI agent calls a company for you: a debt collector, an insurer, your bank. Say here what it may do, sign it, and give the agent the link. Whoever it calls opens the link and sees, in seconds, who sent the agent and what it may agree to.

Nothing is uploaded and this page makes no network requests. The whole signed mandate is inside the link, after the #.
